Character Creation

To help you create a character that is Stockholm D&D Legal, we have included a step-by-step process both for those who use D&DBeyond and those who do not. If you are familiar with D&D 5.5e, you can simply read the summary.

If you are new to D&D, we strongly recommend that you choose a character without spellcasting, i.e. Barbarian or Fighter.

A summary of our character creation rules#

  • Start at level 3.
  • Any official D&D-source from the approved list.
  • Any of the allowed classes and species.
  • Point buy or Standard array. (Rolling for stats is not permitted.)
  • Fixed hit-points.
  • No alignment
  • Starting equipment, one common magic item from the magic items list, + 100gp.
  • Feats and Multi-classing are allowed.
  • You may come from any D&D-esque world, including homebrew; we are setting agnostic.
  • Follow the spirit of the game, have fun together, don’t cheat or try to find loopholes.
  • You are responsible for your character, be mindful of the whole table.

Using D&D Beyond character creator:#

  1. Sources: We support officially published materials (i.e. Player’s Handbook etc.), you can find a full list further down in the document, we do not allow any Unearthed Arcana.
  2. On D&D Beyond, this means deselecting any sources other than Core Rules and Expanded Rules.
  3. Optional Features are permitted.
  4. For Advancement we use Milestone-levelling
  5. Use Fixed hit point type
  6. We use prerequisites for Feats and Multiclass requirements
  7. Character privacy: Public
  8. Choose a Class
  9. Set your level to 3
    Note that multiclassing is allowed.
  10. Select Proficiencies and Class Features
  11. Choose a Background.
    Note that Custom Background isn’t currently supported¹ in 5.5e.
  12. Set your Ability Score Increases
  13. Choose a Species
  14. Select Proficiencies and Species Features
    Ability scores generation method: Point Buy or Standard Array. 1. (Rolling for stats is not permitted.)
    Choose Starting Equipment (you may sell these items at half 1. price)
  15. Under Currency add 100 gold
  16. Choose a common magic item from the list below.
  17. Add appearance, backstory, and other character details.
    Note that we do not use alignment or lifestyle.

¹ In 5.5e it is an optional rule and D&D Beyond doesn’t currently support the 5.5e version. When they do we may permit it.

Without D&D Beyond:#

Sources: We support officially published materials (i.e. Player’s Handbook etc.), you can find a full list further down in the document, we do not allow any Unearthed Arcana.

  1. Choose a Class, starting at level 3
    Note that multiclassing is allowed.
  2. Note down your Proficiencies and Class Features
    Choose Starting Equipment (you may sell these items at half 1. price)
  3. Choose a Background.
  4. Note that Custom Background isn’t currently supported¹ in 5.5e.
  5. Select your Ability Score Increases from your background.
    Ability scores: Point Buy or Standard Array. (Rolling for 1. stats is not permitted.)
  6. Establish your max hit points using the fixed die result (i.e. 5hp for a d8)
  7. Choose a Species
  8. Note down your Proficiencies and Species Features
  9. Gain a 100 gold
  10. Choose a common magic item from the list below.
  11. Add appearance, backstory, and other character details.
  12. Note that we do not use alignment or lifestyle.
  13. Calculate your Carrying Capacity as defined in the Rules 1. Glossary

¹ In 5.5e it is an optional rule and D&D Beyond doesn’t currently support the 5.5e version.When they do we may permit it.

Notes for Druids#

The new 5.5e rules limit the number of known forms you can wildshape into. You can find available beasts in the Monster Manual (MM’25). It is up to you to have the stats available for your chosen beasts.

Notes on Encumbrance and Carrying Capacity#

The specific rules regarding encumbrance have been removed from the 5.5e rules but if you try to lift, move, push, or manipulate a large and/or heavy object the Dungeon Master may refer to the carrying capacity calculations to determine if it is possible, or if you need to make a check.
