Downtime

Downtime#

After your character has survived an adventure, you may perform one downtime activity, these are: Crafting, Learning a new language or tool, Spell scribing, and Buying life insurance.
There is no set time for how long downtime is but it cannot be used for repeated casting of spells, or similar activities, to create permanent effects that may or may not impact game mechanics. To avoid complications downtime activities are limited to the ones described in this document.
To log your downtime, use the &character command after your Dungeon Master has logged the adventure you went on.
Note: This must be done before your next adventure starts. Downtime does not stack.

Crafting - Shop one item for half price#

The basic concept behind crafting is simple, your character has proficiency in tools, and can use them to create items. In mechanical terms what this means is you buy an item at half-price.
You can only use the Crafting-option for one item, the item cost is found in the PHB.
Remember, you must have the tool proficiency and own the toolkit.

Spell scribing#

You may scribe one spell into your spellbook according to the rules found in your class feature regarding paying the gold cost. The one spell may come from a spell scroll that you have bought or earned as a reward, or from another character:
If your last adventure was in the same party as another character with a spellbook, you may copy a spell from their spellbook, provided that they agree.

Learn a new language / tool: 300gp#

Your character may learn any new language or tool proficiency (toolkit not included), but there is one restriction on how many you can learn:

*Sum total of language and tool proficiencies = Intelligence ability score.*

If your character has an intelligence of 8, and you already have a total of 8 language and tool proficiencies, you cannot learn a new one until your intelligence score is increased.
Note: You cannot learn a new skill, saving throw, weapon or armor proficiency with this option.

Silver a Weapon#

You may get a weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition you already own silvered for 100gp.

Buying a Poison#

You may purchase a single dose of poison at the prices listed in the DMG.

Buy Life Insurance#

The Guild offers two forms of life insurance, which are the equivalent of the spells Raise Dead and Resurrection. Provided the character’s body is returned to the Guild and all the requirements of the spells are satisfied, a character who has purchased life insurance will be returned to life should they die during an adventure. When invoked the life insurance policy expires after it is used. The character may purchase a new one if they can afford it.

Life Insurance PolicyCost in gold (gp)
Raise Dead1250
Resurrection2500

If you die on an adventure and are returned to life by a life insurance policy you have purchased, you do not gain any of the benefits of completing that adventure, such as gold, half a level, or downtime. However, you do not suffer any of the consequences of resurrection either since it is assumed that sufficient time has passed before your next adventure to negate any side-effects.

If you are unhappy with your character#

We recognize that sometimes you would like to go back in time and change a thing or two, and before your character reaches level 5, you may change a few things with your character:

  • Subspecies
  • Subclass (Including School, Patron, etc)
  • Class features (Invocations, fighting styles, metamagic, etc.)
  • Background
  • Feats
  • Ability score increases
  • Chosen spells
  • Name, appearance, other descriptive factors
