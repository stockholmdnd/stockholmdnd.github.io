Playing your first game

Playing your first game#

Make sure your character is Guild-legal, if you are unsure about it feel free to share the character or any questions in the channel #beginner-help. Before you go on your first adventure, your character may sell any unwanted items from their starting equipment for half the listed price (PHB), and may buy any item listed in the PHB or further down in this document.
You can purchase any item found in the PHB for the listed price, as well as items found in this document under the “Shopping”-section.
You should know who your character is, being able to describe what they look like is nice, but you are responsible for knowing the mechanics of your character: Know what your species and class features, as well as spells and other abilities, do before the game starts. Nobody wants to look at the rules mid-game.

Registering for an event#

Events will be posted either as a separate event for our recurring events, or as a floating-session. To join an event as a player, click the green check mark. If you cannot attend, click the red cross. This is especially important if you have signed up and then cannot attend. Clicking the red cross frees up your seat for someone else on the wait list. The red D&D ampersand is for Dungeon Masters, only click it if you intend to host a table at the event.
After you have signed into an event as a player you need to join a table. The Dungeon Masters will post tables in the event channel. Pick a table with open spots for which you have a character of the corresponding tier. Join the table by clicking the waving hand. When you do, the bot will notify the Dungeon Master who in turn will admit you, provided there are spaces left. When you are admitted to a table the bot will send a DM to you with instructions for submitting your character. Follow the instructions and confirm the character’s participation at the end. After that all you have to do is show up at the event.
Pro tip; if you have unused downtime, resolve that before submitting your character.

Life Insurance - a Guild benefit#

A character created in Stockholm D&D starts with a Raise Dead life insurance policy, meaning that if they die, they get a second chance. In order to invoke the life insurance policy your party must return your character’s body from the adventure. You can read more about life insurance in the chapter Buy Life Insurance.

Our regular events are on the first Saturday of every month (barring holidays or the venue being fully booked by other events) and are held at Alphaspel with sessions announced at least two weeks in advance. Every member of The Guild is allowed to host floating sessions (more info can be found in our documentation and in our server).
Alphaspel has the following rules that we need to follow:

  1. All participants neet to purchase a 50sek token form the store to cover the rent of the rooms. The tokens are turned in to the DMs who then return them to the store at the end of the session.
  2. No outside food or drink is allowed in the room in the days the kitchen is open, which is all days.
  3. Alcohol is only allowed in the bar areas. Signage is clearly visible on the borders of those areas up and must be followed.
  4. Rooms are booked 10-16 and should be vacated on time to accommodate other patrons that have booked them after us.

Other official sessions might be posted periodically on the server by the architects. Other members are also welcome to create floating sessions and play as they please.
