Rewards

Every adventure you play in Stockholm D&D is worth half a level, as long as your character survives the adventure.

You start at level 3. After surviving 2 adventures (0.5 levels each) you will be level 4.

After each adventure, the Dungeon Master decides how much gold the party gets, based on the tier played:

Acquiring loot on adventures#

You may acquire permanent and consumable magic items in your adventures. Permanent magic items acquired in a session can never be carried out of that session. Consumable magic items can, but this must be explicitly stated by the Dungeon Master, who should specify what the item is and how many times it can be used before it is consumed.

Consumable magic items awarded in this way may or may not be used in future sessions. When you sign up for a session and have received consumable magic items from a previous session, check with the Dungeon Master if you can use those at their table.

To ensure balance, reduce social pressure, and make life easier for Dungeon Masters, your character cannot keep or sell loot they found in an adventure. The Dungeon Masters and the Guild award any and all rewards.

Rule
Pressuring your Dungeon Master for more gold or better consumables is against our rules.

Permanent magic items#

At certain levels, your character gains 1 permanent magic item from the Guild. The full list is in the magic items catalogue.

LevelRarity
5Uncommon
9Rare
13Very Rare
17Legendary

When you become eligible for a magic item at a given level of rarity, you may exchange a magic item you chose at a previous rarity for another item of that same earlier rarity.

Example: Bob the Fighter chose a +1 Greataxe from the uncommon list at level 5. Upon reaching level 9, he picks a +2 Greataxe from the rare list and exchanges the +1 Greataxe he got at level 5 for a Ring of Jumping from the uncommon list.

You cannot purchase or craft magic items, nor can you keep permanent magic items found during a session.

Special magic armor and firearms rule#

To acquire magic armor or a magic firearm, you must first own the mundane version of that item.
